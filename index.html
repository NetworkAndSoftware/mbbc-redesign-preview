A new look for mtbakerbikeclub.org

The proposed redesign of our website, side by side with the site we have today. A site that looks inviting gets people to look around: to find a ride, to join, and to come back. It's ready in ClubExpress as a saved draft. Nothing changes on the live site until the board approves it.

Still ClubExpress

Same system, same pages, calendar, membership and sign-up. No new services or costs. The current design stays saved, so we can switch back.

Our colors

Fir, kelly and chartreuse greens from the club jersey. The hero shows Mt. Baker at sunrise, drawn from the mountain's real outline as seen across Bellingham Bay.

Clear about who we are

The club as it really is: rides for every level, skills and Ride Leader training, and the Chuckanut Classic giving back. No more promises of racing.

2Club rides stand out on the calendar

Today the community rides we list as a service, like the Rabbit and Donut rides, get the brightest color, while the club's own rides are blue and grey. The new calendar turns that around: rides the club leads are solid green, and rides it doesn't run fade into pale tints.

3Weekly rides at a glance

One long page of text becomes a card per ride: where, when, how far and how fast, and whether it's running now, seasonal, or paused until a Ride Leader steps up. Rides the club doesn't run are listed separately.

4One place for our online channels

Our Facebook pages, Strava club, route library and kit store are linked here and there, or not at all. A new Find us online page under About gathers them in one place and says what each one is for, and the footer on every page links to it.

5Bellingham, a cycling paradise

The club grew out of a love of riding here, and the old site said so: it called Bellingham "a veritable cycling paradise" and gave it a page. The new homepage brings that back in a Where we ride band, and the page itself is rewritten as Cycling in Bellingham: the welcome sign that calls Bellingham a Bicycle Friendly Community, a map of where we are, where to ride, the bike shops, the groups that make this a great place to ride, and a hello for newcomers.

Good to know

  • "Who we are" is a draft for the board to agree on: a few words about our members, then Ride · Learn · Give, the proposed homepage version of the mission statement.
  • The 2027 Chuckanut Classic date and the dues amounts on the new homepage still need confirming.
  • Every page gets the new header and footer, including pages not shown here.
  • These screenshots show what a visitor sees on a computer. Members see Member Center and Help Out in the menu, as they do today.
  • Most pages are edited the same way as today. The new homepage sections and the Weekly Rides cards are HTML blocks, so changing their text takes a little more care.
